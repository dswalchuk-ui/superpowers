# 5. Weekly Rhythm & Success Metrics

A sustainable cadence around a full-time job, and simple metrics per phase so you
can see progress and not quit when month one isn't a windfall.

---

## The weekly rhythm

The failure mode isn't lack of ideas — it's an unsustainable pace that collapses
after two good weeks. So the rhythm is **small, fixed, and boring on purpose.**

### The rule: two blocks + a daily 5 minutes

| When | Block | Time | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| One weeknight | **Content session** | 60–90 min | Doc 4: turn one asset into a week of scheduled posts. |
| One weekend slot | **Business session** | 1–2 hrs | The current phase's "one must-finish thing" (build the store, list a title, set up email, etc.). |
| Daily | **Show up** | ~5 min | Reply to comments/DMs, glance at orders. Ship in the weekend block. |

**That's it. ~3 hours a week.** Two calendar blocks. Put them in your calendar as
real recurring appointments — treat them like a shift you can't skip.

### The one-must-finish rule (this is the Section 9 mechanism)
Every week has **exactly one "must-finish" thing.** Not a list — one. Examples:
- Week 1: Payhip account + first draft product.
- Week 2: first title published (physical + ebook).
- Week 3: Instagram live + first week of posts scheduled.
- Week 4: email capture live + first video listed.

Finishing the one thing = the week was a win, full stop. Everything else is bonus.
**Finishing small things repeatedly is the entire point of this project.**

### Protecting against the quit pattern
- **Miss a week? Not failure.** Resume the next block. The system is designed to
  survive gaps — scheduled content keeps running even when you don't touch it.
- **When motivation dips, shrink the task, don't skip it.** "Publish a title" too
  big tonight? Just "write the product description." Momentum > volume.
- **The archive does the heavy lifting.** You're not creating from scratch. On low
  days, remember the hard part (900+ clinics, decades of material) is already done.

## Success metrics by phase

Keep score, but score the **right** things. Early on, measure **activity you
control** (did the thing get built/posted?), not **revenue you don't** (sales are a
lagging result of months of funnel-building). Section 6 of the brief is explicit:
this builds to a few hundred $/month over time, not overnight. Metrics reflect that.

### Phase 0 (this week) — *did the foundation get gathered?*
- [ ] First titles confirmed with Stan (yes/no)
- [ ] Inventory in hand + rough count (yes/no)
- [ ] Print files / covers / ISBNs collected — or gap identified (yes/no)

**Success = all three are yes/known.** No revenue metric here.

### Phase 1 (weeks 1–4) — *is the storefront real and live?*
- Store live with **1 title** (physical + ebook): yes/no
- First video listed digitally: yes/no
- Instagram live + **first week of posts scheduled**: yes/no
- Email capture live: yes/no
- **First sale of any kind** (even one — proof the pipe works end to end)

**Success = store is live and the first sale happened.** One sale is the milestone,
not the amount.

### Phase 2 (months 2–3) — *is the engine turning on its own?*
- Posting **consistently** (target ~4–6 posts/week via the batch system): yes/no
- **Titles 2–3** live: yes/no
- Email list **growing** (track the number, direction matters more than size)
- **First newsletter** sent: yes/no
- POD set up and ready to switch on: yes/no
- **Trend:** are monthly sales > $0 and generally rising month over month?

**Success = the content engine runs weekly without heroics, and sales exist and
trend up.** Absolute dollars still secondary.

### Phase 3 (later) — *is there durable, growing side income?*
- Monthly revenue trending toward **a few hundred $/month** (the brief's realistic
  target)
- Audience growing → some **YouTube ad revenue** starting
- Merch and/or affiliate links live (the small upside)

**Success = a real, growing side income tied to Stan's work that you've sustained
for months.** That sustained-follow-through *is* the win the brief is really after.

## The single most important metric
> **Weeks in a row you hit your one must-finish thing.**

Track that number somewhere visible. It's the leading indicator of every other
metric, and it's the direct measure of the pattern you're trying to break. A long
streak of small finishes is the whole game.
