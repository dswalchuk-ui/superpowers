## The one-paragraph model

Books were free for operational reasons, not lack of demand (900+ clinic
participants over 29 years is the proof). You're rebuilding it as a promoted paid
business: sell existing hard copies yourself now, switch physical books to
print-on-demand once stock runs out, sell ebooks and video digitally for instant
delivery, keep some content free as the funnel. Stan and Marlene run none of it.
Old site retired once the new store is live and stable.

## The roadmap (from the brief, condensed)

- **Phase 0 — this week:** confirm first titles with Stan, get inventory to your
  place with a rough count, gather print-ready files + cover files + ISBNs.
- **Phase 1 — weeks 1–4:** store live, first title listed (physical + ebook),
  first video converted + listed, Instagram created + first posts, email capture.
- **Phase 2 — months 2–3:** content engine running weekly, titles 2–3 live, POD
  set up and ready, first newsletter, plan old-site shutdown.
- **Phase 3 — later:** branded merch, gear/tack affiliate links.

## ▶ START HERE — the first two moves

Everything downstream depends on these. They run in parallel.

### Track A — the phone call to Stan (only you can do this; do it this week)
This is the true first mover. Nothing gets listed without it.

- [ ] Confirm **Trail Riding & Packing (book + video)** is the first title.
- [ ] Arrange to get the remaining hard-copy inventory to your place.
- [ ] Get a **rough count per title** (even ballpark — "about 40 Trail Riding").
- [ ] Ask Stan/Marlene for the **print-ready interior files, cover files, and
      ISBNs** for each title (needed for ebooks now and POD later). If they only
      have printed books and no files, note that — it changes the ebook plan
      (see doc 3, "if you have no source files").

### Track B — the platform + first listing (you can start now, before inventory arrives)
- [ ] Read [`01-platform-recommendation.md`](01-platform-recommendation.md).
- [ ] Create the **free Payhip account** (the recommended starting platform — $0,
      no risk).
- [ ] Create **one draft product**: the Trail Riding & Packing book. Placeholder
      price, placeholder cover — just to learn the flow. Don't publish yet.

When Track A delivers the inventory count and files, you flip the draft to live.
**That is Phase 1's first win.**

## Why the platform pick is what it is (short version)

Full reasoning is in doc 1. The headline: **start on Payhip (free, $0/month), move
to Shopify only once you're consistently selling.** Paying ~$50/month for Shopify
before a single sale is exactly the kind of upfront commitment that makes month-one
feel like failure and triggers the quit pattern. Payhip lets you launch a real,
paid product this month at zero fixed cost, prove the follow-through, and graduate
to the fuller platform when there's revenue to justify it.
