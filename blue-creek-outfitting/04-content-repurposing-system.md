# 4. Content Repurposing System

Turn **one** video clip or **one** magazine article into **a week of scheduled
posts** — step by step, with tools. Built to run in one or two evening sessions a
week, not daily.

*Tools confirmed July 2026; sources at bottom.*

---

## The principle

You already own the hardest part: a deep archive (42 magazine articles, hours of
footage, YouTube back-catalogue, the Bear Blog). You are **not creating content —
you are cutting existing content into pieces and scheduling them.** That reframe is
what makes this sustainable around a job.

**One source asset → one week of posts. Batch a month in one sitting when you can.**

---

## The tool stack (free/low-cost to start)

| Job | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule + auto-post | **Buffer** (free: 3 channels, 10 queued/channel) or **Metricool** (free: 1 brand, incl. analytics + TikTok) | Free |
| Clip long video → shorts w/ captions | **Opus Clip** or **CapCut** (auto-captions, free tiers) | Free/low |
| Simple graphics / carousels / quote cards | **Canva** (free) | Free |
| Native scheduling fallback | **Meta Business Suite** (unlimited FB+IG scheduling) | Free |

Start with **Buffer + CapCut + Canva.** Add Metricool later if you want analytics
in one place. Don't shop for tools for three weeks — pick these and go.

## Recipe A — one VIDEO CLIP → a week of posts

Say you pull a 3–5 minute segment from the Trail Riding & Packing video (e.g. "how
to throw a diamond hitch").

1. **Extract the segment** from the master/YouTube (any editor, or just note the
   timestamps).
2. **Auto-cut to shorts:** run it through CapCut/Opus Clip → get 2–3 vertical
   (9:16) clips, 20–60s each, with **auto-captions burned in** (most people watch
   muted).
3. **Make supporting posts from the same segment:**
   - 1× **quote/tip card** in Canva (a single sentence of advice from the clip).
   - 1× **still photo** from the footage with a one-line caption.
   - 1× **text/carousel post** — "3 mistakes people make with the diamond hitch."
4. **Write captions once, adapt lightly per platform.** Every caption ends with the
   **same call to action → the store link** ("Full technique is in the Trail Riding
   & Packing book — link in bio").
5. **Schedule across the week** in Buffer:

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Short clip #1 (Reel/Short/TikTok) |
| Tue | Quote/tip card |
| Wed | Short clip #2 |
| Thu | Still photo + tip |
| Fri | Carousel ("3 mistakes…") |
| Sat | Short clip #3 or repost best performer |

That's **6 posts from one segment**, cross-posted to Instagram + YouTube Shorts +
Facebook (+ TikTok if you want it).

## Recipe B — one MAGAZINE ARTICLE → a week of posts

One of the 42 articles (e.g. a piece on trailering safety).

1. **Pull 5–7 key points** from the article (skim, list the takeaways).
2. Turn each into a post:
   - 1× **carousel** in Canva — each slide = one takeaway (great on Instagram).
   - 3× **single tip cards** (one point each).
   - 1× **quote graphic** of the best line.
   - 1× **"link to read the full free article" post** (drives to the free archive on
     your site → email capture → store). *The free articles are the funnel — always
     point to them.*
3. If you have a photo that matches, pair it; otherwise Canva templates.
4. Same CTA discipline: every post points to either the **free article** (top of
   funnel) or the **book** (bottom of funnel).
5. Schedule Mon–Sat in Buffer, same rhythm as Recipe A.

## The repeatable weekly workflow (your actual routine)

**One "content session" per week, ~60–90 min:**
1. Pick **one** source asset (rotate: video clip one week, article the next).
2. Run the recipe → produce ~6 posts.
3. Drop them all into Buffer, scheduled across the coming week.
4. Close the laptop. Buffer posts them automatically. You're done for the week.

**Batching upgrade (once the routine is comfortable):** do this for 4 assets in one
longer Sunday session = **a month of content** scheduled in ~3 hours. This is the
real time-saver and the thing that survives busy weeks.

**Engagement (5 min/day, optional but valuable):** reply to comments/DMs. That's
what actually grows a niche account — you don't need to *post* daily, just *show up*
in replies.

---

## Guardrails so this stays sustainable
- **Never post live/manually as the system.** If it's not scheduled in Buffer, it
  doesn't happen. Manual posting is what burns people out.
- **Recycle winners.** A short that did well 8 weeks ago is new to most of today's
  feed. Re-queue it.
- **One CTA per post, always to the store or the free article.** Content without a
  path to the store is a hobby, not a funnel.
- **Don't chase every platform.** Start with **Instagram (Reels) + YouTube Shorts**,
  since you already have YouTube. Add TikTok only if it's easy.
