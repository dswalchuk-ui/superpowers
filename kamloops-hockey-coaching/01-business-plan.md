# Kamloops Hockey Skills Coaching
## Launch Package v1.0 | Prepared July 13, 2026 | Target first revenue: September 2026

---

## 1. The Business in One Paragraph

Pro-experience skills coaching for Kamloops minor hockey players, built around
three products that don't require you to carry ice costs alone: team skills
sessions (the team's ice, your expertise), small-group dryland/shooting
training (no ice needed), and semi-private on-ice groups (5+ skaters so the
group clears the ice bill). Target: $1,200 to $1,800/month gross by November
on 4 to 6 hours per week.

---

## 2. Positioning

**One-liner:** "Skills development from a coach who's played every level your
player is aiming for: WHL, university, and pro in North America and Europe."

**Why it wins in Kamloops:** This is a WHL town. Parents of rep players pay
for credibility and player development pathways. Almost no local competitor
can say they've played pro in Europe and trained at KHL-level pace. Your
estimating day job is irrelevant here; your playing resume is the entire brand.

**Target clients, in priority order:**

1. **KMHA rep team coaches/managers (U11 to U18)** who want a skills
   specialist to run segments of their already-booked practices. Team pays
   from team budget. Zero ice cost to you.
2. **Parents of rep players** for weekly dryland/shooting groups (fall/winter)
   and semi-private ice groups.
3. **Spring hockey programs and academies** (March to June). This is the
   biggest private-pay season in BC. Book in January/February.
4. Adult skills groups: only if slots are empty. Low priority.

## 3. Service Menu and Pricing

| Product | Price | Your ice cost | Margin/hr | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Team skills session (their ice, 60 min) | $200 flat | $0 | ~$200 | The wedge product. Run power skating/skills segment within team practice |
| Team skills package (8 sessions) | $1,440 ($180/session) | $0 | ~$180 | Locks a team for half a season |
| Dryland + shooting group (60 min, 6–10 athletes) | $30/athlete | $0 | $180–$300 | Garage gym, shooting pads, sticks and balls. Year-round |
| Semi-private ice (60 min, 6–8 skaters) | $55/skater | ~$200–$280 | $50–$240 | Only run at 6+ confirmed and prepaid skaters (6 × $55 = $330 clears worst-case ice). Below 6, cancel or convert to dryland |
| Private 1-on-1 (on client-provided ice or stacked before/after a group) | $100/hr | $0 | $100 | Never rent a full sheet for one skater |
| Video/skills assessment add-on | $50 | $0 | $50 | Film session, 10-min voice-note breakdown. Uses your existing AI tooling |

**Pricing rationale:** Kamloops market for ex-pro instruction supports $90 to
$125/hr private and $150 to $250 team sessions. These numbers position you
mid-premium: credible, not greedy, easy yes for a team budget. Validate
against 2 or 3 local competitors' posted rates before printing anything.

**Hard rule:** never rent ice speculatively. Ice gets booked only after a
group is paid or a team provides it.

**Payment and cancellation policy (print it, enforce it):**

- Packages and group blocks prepaid by e-transfer before session 1.
- Individual sessions: 24-hour cancellation notice or the session is charged.
- Team packages invoiced 50% at booking, 50% at session 4.
- No refunds for athlete no-shows; one make-up session per block for
  illness/injury with notice.

## 4. Ice Strategy

- City of Kamloops books arena ice through Recreation Services
  (250-828-3500). Confirm current private rental hourly rates and fall
  availability windows before quoting any on-ice group. Assume $200 to
  $280/hr prime until confirmed.
- Fall prime ice is scarce (minor hockey and junior teams absorb it). That
  scarcity is exactly why the team-session product leads: KMHA teams already
  hold the ice.
- Non-prime slots (early morning, late evening, weekend gaps) are your
  semi-private windows.
- Summer 2027: cheaper ice, camps and 3-on-3 season. Plan a July skills camp
  then, not now.

## 6. Client Acquisition Plan

**August = booking month.** Rep team tryouts and season planning happen late
August/September. Coaches are deciding their season budgets right now.

**Channel 1: KMHA team coaches (the wedge)**
Get the rep coach/manager contact list via your existing hockey network, then
send the template in [03-outreach-templates.md](03-outreach-templates.md).
Offer every team one free 30-minute demo segment inside a practice they
already have booked. One good demo in front of 17 kids converts to a paid
package and 17 sets of parents who now know your name.

**Channel 2: Your network + Instagram**

- One 45-second reel: you skating/shooting at pace, resume on screen,
  "booking fall skills groups."
- Boost $50 within 25 km of Kamloops, parent demographics.
- Post in local hockey parent Facebook groups (get admin permission first).

**Channel 3: Spring hockey (January action item)**
List Kamloops-area spring programs in January, pitch skills-coach roles for
March to June. Calendar reminder: January 15, 2027.

## 10. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Injury/liability | CGL insurance + signed waivers before any athlete steps on ice or turf. No exceptions, no "just this once" |
| Fall ice scarcity | Team-ice wedge and dryland products carry the season; you rent almost nothing |
| Work travel/site conflicts | Fixed weekly slots (e.g., Tue/Thu 6–8pm). Never promise daytime availability |
| Seasonality (April–June dip in minor hockey) | Spring hockey pipeline booked in January covers it |
| Undercharging drift | Prices printed on the one-pager. Discount = package structure only, never hourly rate cuts |
| Reputation risk (this is a small hockey town) | Over-deliver the free demos. Every session is an audition in front of 17 families |
