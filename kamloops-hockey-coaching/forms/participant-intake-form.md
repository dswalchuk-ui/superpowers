### Consents — LANGUAGE TO BE DRAFTED BY LAWYER

The lawyer should draft, at minimum, provisions covering:

1. **Informed consent / assumption of risk** — acknowledgment of the inherent
   risks of hockey and off-ice training activities.
2. **Release of liability and indemnification** — scope appropriate for a
   sole-proprietor sport instructor in BC, referencing the CGL policy.
3. **Emergency medical treatment authorization** — consent for the coach to
   seek emergency medical care if the parent/guardian cannot be reached.
4. **Photo/video consent** — separate opt-in checkbox; sessions may be filmed
   for the video assessment product and occasionally for social media.
   Social-media use must be individually opt-in, not bundled.
5. **Personal information handling** — how medical and contact information is
   stored and when it is destroyed (BC PIPA applies to businesses collecting
   personal information).
