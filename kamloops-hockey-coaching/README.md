# Kamloops Hockey Skills Coaching — Launch Package v1.0

Prepared July 13, 2026 · Target first revenue: September 2026

Pro-experience skills coaching for Kamloops minor hockey players, built around
three products that don't require carrying ice costs alone: team skills
sessions (the team's ice, your expertise), small-group dryland/shooting
training (no ice needed), and semi-private on-ice groups (5+ skaters so the
group clears the ice bill).

**Target: $1,200–$1,800/month gross by November on 4–6 hours per week.**

## The three hard rules

1. **Never rent ice speculatively.** Ice gets booked only after a group is
   paid or a team provides it.
2. **No athlete on ice or turf without insurance in place and a signed
   waiver.** No exceptions, no "just this once."
3. **Prices are printed on the rate sheet.** Discount = package structure
   only, never hourly rate cuts.

## Do this first (week of Aug 3)

1. Submit the BC online criminal record check (working-with-minors category).
2. Request CGL insurance quotes from two brokers ($2M minimum, sport instruction).
3. Send the waiver/intake draft to a lawyer.
4. Apply for the City of Kamloops home-based business licence.
5. Start filling in the coach outreach tracker from your hockey network.
