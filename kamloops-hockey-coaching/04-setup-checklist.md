- [ ] **City of Kamloops business licence** — home-based service category.
- [ ] **Sole proprietorship** — operate under your own name to start;
      register a trade name only if you want one.
- [ ] **Separate bank account** for the business. E-transfer +
      Square/Stripe invoicing.
- [ ] **Expense tracking** — every expense (ice, insurance, gear, mileage)
      goes in [trackers/expense-tracker.csv](trackers/expense-tracker.csv);
      all deductible against the income.
- [ ] **GST awareness** — registration not required until $30K/yr revenue
      across **ALL self-employment income combined** (this business +
      estimating). Watch the combined number quarterly.
