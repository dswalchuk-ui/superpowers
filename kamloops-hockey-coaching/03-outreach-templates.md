> Worth a quick call this week?

> Dylan
> [phone]

**Fields to fill:** `[U15]` division, `[Coach name]`, `[Mutual contact]`,
`[phone]`.

## 7c. Follow-up bump (3 days after 7a, no reply)

**When to send:** Exactly 3 days after the first message. One bump only —
after that, move them to "circle back October" in the tracker.

> Hi [Coach name], quick bump on the free skills segment. My September slots
> are filling with [X] teams so far. If the timing's wrong this month, happy
> to circle back for October.

**Fields to fill:** `[Coach name]`, `[X]` (real number of committed teams —
never inflate it; this is a small hockey town and coaches talk).
